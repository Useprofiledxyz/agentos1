How it works

An agent that reads, checks
and waits for your signature.

01

Read

Live pools across Uniswap, Pons, Long, Bankr, Virtuals and more. Prices, liquidity, balances and contract bytecode, straight from Robinhood Chain. Read tools cannot move funds.

02

Check

The Checkpoint quotes the trade, sells it back, reads the contract, simulates the exact transaction and applies your limits. A failed check means no ticket.

03

Sign

The ticket is re-checked on the live pool the moment you press sign, rebuilt from a fresh route, and sent from your own wallet with a hard minimum-out.

The Checkpoint

Nine checks.
Every trade.
No exceptions.

The same checks run on every quote, again when the ticket is built, and again right before your wallet signs. They live outside the model: the agent cannot talk its way past them.

Check a token →
  1. RouteAn executable route exists, through the known router only.
  2. Test sellSelling back what you'd receive goes through. Catches honeypots and transfer taxes.
  3. Price impact + feesWhat the trade really costs, one way, against your limit.
  4. Vs market priceFlags pools that sell far above the market price.
  5. ContractBytecode heuristics: mint, pause, blacklist, fee setters, proxies, live owners.
  6. GasGas as a share of the trade.
  7. SimulationThe exact transaction succeeds from your address.
  8. Your limitsPer-trade and daily caps, slippage, pause, blocked tokens.
  9. Operator stopOne switch halts all execution for everyone.

Questions

Before you connect.

Does Quill hold my funds or keys?

No. The server builds unsigned transactions; your wallet signs them. Sign-in is a free signature over a one-time message and cannot move funds.

Can the agent trade without me?

No. The agent can only prepare tickets. Every ticket waits for you to review it and sign it in your own wallet.

Is the Checkpoint an audit?

No. It is a set of live checks and bytecode heuristics. It catches common traps (honeypots, taxes, thin pools, owner powers) but cannot prove a token is safe.

Which chains and tokens?

Robinhood Chain first: ETH, WETH, USDG, tokenized stocks and any coin by address. Base, Arbitrum and Ethereum are available too.

Why was my trade blocked?

The ticket shows which check failed and why: no route back, a high round-trip loss, a price far from market, or a limit. Nothing is built when a check fails.
