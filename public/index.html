The desk

A pen is only dangerous in the wrong hand.

So Quill never holds it. The agent reads Solana for you: the pools on Raydium, Meteora and Orca, every pump.fun and LaunchLab launch from the last minute, the price of NVDAx on chain. It quotes, it sells the trade back to itself, it reads the contract, it applies your limits. Then it writes a ticket and puts the pen in your hand.

IThe Checkpoint

Every trade, checked twice.

Before a ticket exists, and again the moment you press sign, the Checkpoint quotes the route, sells the trade back, reads the contract for mint, blacklist and tax switches, simulates the exact transaction and applies your limits. Fail one, no ticket. The panel on the right is running live on today's top coin.

Questions

Before you connect.

Does Quill hold my funds or keys?

No. The server builds unsigned transactions and your wallet signs them. Signing in is a free signature over a one-time message; it cannot move funds.

Can the agent trade without me?

No. The agent can only prepare tickets. Each one waits for you to review and sign it in your own wallet, and expires after 10 minutes.

Is the Checkpoint an audit?

No. It is a set of live checks and bytecode heuristics. It catches common traps (honeypots, taxes, thin pools, owner powers) but cannot prove a token is safe.

Which chains and tokens?

Solana: SOL, USDC, USDT, every SPL and Token-2022 coin by its mint (pump.fun and LaunchLab launches included) and xStocks, tokenized stocks and ETFs.

Why was my trade blocked?

The ticket shows which check failed and why: no route back, a high round-trip loss, a price far from market, or one of your limits. Nothing is built when a check fails.
